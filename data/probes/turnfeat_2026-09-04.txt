Turn-arithmetic features (panel 2, rank 5) -- staged protocol
2026-09-04.  Stage 1 declared before the probe: build a turn-arithmetic
feature block only if the policy's endgame error against the exact
solver is elevated in the turn-sensitive buckets by more than the
bucket's binomial SE.

Stage 1 (tools/parityprobe, perfect-information solver labels on 3,342
raw-policy self-play states at deck <= 5; full table in
data/probes/parity_main_2026-09-04.txt):
  raw policy top-1 error vs the solver: 11.5% overall, mean loss 1.18
  pts per state.
  deck parity: odd (mover draws last) 9.8% vs even 13.7% -- the odd
    bucket is LOWER by 5.6 SE: parity alone is not the deficit.
  nplay > turns_left (A) 15.1% vs nplay <= turns_left (B) 4.7%: A is
    elevated by 13.5 bucket SE (mean loss 1.57 vs 0.45); it stays
    elevated with the pile-draw cases removed (6.7% vs 2.0% at deck<=3).
  the concentration: states where the solver's best is a pure stall
    (an unplayable pile card taken to flip who draws last, 6.3% of
    states, wanted almost only at even deck counts): 53.6% error, 7.1
    pts per miss -- about half of all endgame errors and 60% of the
    point loss.  The policy also over-stalls in odd states (11.6% of
    its top-1s vs 2.0% wanted).
  Verdict on the declared rule: PROCEED on the A-vs-B criterion; the
  block to build is turns_left (both sides), nplay, nplay - turns_left,
  and who-draws-last-if-I-stall, not a bare parity bit.
Caveats recorded: the labels know both hands AND the deck order, so
stall labels are an upper bound (a stall can be "best" only because
the next deck card is known); exhaustion dropped 26% of deck-5 states.
The record (README, solver-vote endgame) also says decision-time
exactness at deck <= 5 did not convert to match wins because those
games are mostly decided -- the training value of the block lies in
generalizing turn awareness to deck 6-8.

Stage 1b results (tools/parityprobe -A <tournament string> -P 8; full
tables in data/probes/parity_agent_2026-09-04.txt):
  (b) stall labels are genuine turn arithmetic: of 141 deck<=3 states
      whose solver-best is a pure stall, 136 (96.5%) keep the stall
      optimal under >= 6 of 8 random reorderings of the undrawn deck
      (deck 2: 113/113 under both orders).  Raw policy error on that
      robust set 41.9% +/- 4.2 (5.4 pts per miss); the deployed search
      agent 9.6% +/- 2.5.
  (a) the deployed search agent on the identical states (2,571):
      error 6.3% vs the raw policy's 9.9% (paired +3.5 +/- 0.5); it
      fixes deck <= 2 (1.0% / 2.4% residual) and the robust stalls, but
      NOT deck 3-5 (12.2% -> 11.1%, within noise), and the turn-pressure
      bucket stays elevated after search: 7.9% +/- 0.7 vs 3.4% +/- 0.6
      (6.9 bucket SE).  Residual perfect-information loss after search:
      ~1.0 pt per decision at deck 3-5 vs 0.16 at deck <= 2 (an upper
      bound: the solver also knows the hidden hand).  Mechanism note:
      with the policy-only candidate set a stall the policy does not
      rank in its top five cannot be found by the search; the gen-7
      labeler (eval_cand 4, draw expansion on) can, so gen-7 corrections
      at deck <= 8 -- 69% of which survive confirmation -- are the
      training signal for exactly these states.
