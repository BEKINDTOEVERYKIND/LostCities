## Synthesis: why raw-policy gains do not move the search agent

The five lenses converge on one estimator-side answer with a measurement-side corollary; the evidence is now mostly measured rather than inferred.

1. **The decision is made in the estimator's leaves, and leaf quality is not raw strength.** V-ctl: a net the standing agent beats 73.5% head-to-head, placed ONLY in the playouts (root unchanged), costs 5.75 points of win rate and 9.9 points of margin. Gate V: the v6 turn-aware fine-tune (+2.6 raw points, endgame held-out CE -0.070 at 17x seed spread) in the same slot reads 50.56%, margin +0.17. The leaves carry the decision; "better raw argmax" does not order them (data/probes/playout_slot_2026-09-05.txt).

2. **Corrections labels are the search's own picks, so the raw arena counts fixes the search already makes.** Measured this round on data/corr21c+corr22c (1,356 confirmed gen-7 corrections, refute_transfer2 / candset replays): the label is inside the tournament candidate set on 95.6% (79.6% of the gen-8 corpus, rank histogram 43/181/181/106/91/0/0/41 — zero labels at rank 5-6 by construction, since the labeler's root_width is the same top-5); a single 96-world tournament-string stream already plays the label on 63% of searched-ply corrections; 85-86% of confirmed late labels clear the sel_k gate at 96 worlds in expectation. Only ~7% of labels are below ply 14 (where a prior change transfers 1:1), and that channel's ceiling is bounded by the plylo-8 read (51.3% +/- 2.0 pooled over 2400 games). This is the mechanism behind c9 (52-55% raw -> 49.6% search), the x-head (51.0 -> 49.25), c18 (53.5 -> 51.9) and the gen-7/8 plateau (52.6-52.9% raw, margins +3.5 to +7.0, wins flat).

3. **Objective and frame changes at the leaves flip picks among candidates whose difference is inside the estimator's noise.** Gate W (per-world relabeled playouts, 31% pick flips on stored decisions): 49.62%, margin -1.65 — the removable frame main effect was measured at 0.19 SE^2 (equivalent to ~114 worlds, inside the closed resolution family). Gate X (smooth match-win objective, 16-24% final-round flips): 49.56%, margin -1.90; the out-of-sample V gain per flip was +0.0009 +/- 0.0026 at 96 worlds and -0.0082 +/- 0.0032 at 512. Together with dets 192 (51.4%), sel_deep (51.69%), racing (51.66% / 1600) the record now says: at fixed compute the statistically efficient points argmax is not beaten by more resolution, a different frame, or the theoretically right objective.

4. **Inference is capped.** Bound U: perfect knowledge of the opponent's hand in every world is worth +3.61 pts and 53.50%. No belief-side mechanism (specialist capacity, likelihood correction, corpus re-fit) can be expected to clear 52% alone; the belief direction is closed at this search.

5. **Capacity and RL are not binding.** The same 50-52 dead a2 units sit on every net since c13, including c17 during its +8.8-margin gate win; the fine-tune curves show a memorization gap (train CE 1.34 vs held-out 1.62) that is label-limited. The PPO "low-entropy start" premise is false: data/best_pre_gp.bin == c13 (1.42 nats, the same diffuse regime as c20), and the shaped finishing runs from it sharpened to 0.63 then 0.47 nats at raw parity with gates 50.3/50.8/51.06% — the entropy trajectory the proposal wanted to measure is on disk.

6. **Measurement corollary.** The 800-game "observed >= 52%" rule is a minimum-effect design: P(pass) is 0.08 at true 0, 0.36 at +1.5%, 0.50 at +2%, 0.76 at +3%; more games do not rescue anything under ~2%. Raw-to-search conversion of ~0.5 puts every 52-54% raw net in the invisible band by construction, and the chunk-level slope of wins on margin (0.46-0.71 %/pt, r 0.6-0.9 between gates) coexists with systematic decoupling exactly where near-miss reads occur (Gate L 52.56% at +0.38; Gate M 51.69% at +3.62; Gate K 50.38% at -2.00; sel_k 53.8% at -6.7). The one adoption in the current chain that cleared 52% (bel_samp, Gate L) has never been replicated.

Consequence for this round: no proposal on the table has a measured path to +2%; the highest-value CPU goes to (i) making every future gate cheaper to interpret (CRN + honest paired statistics), (ii) the two direct reads the record has never taken — a gen-8 net as MAIN net at the tournament string, and an estimator-error instrument for the leaf role that the V-ctl disposition names as the family's one live arm — and (iii) calibrating the regime itself on its own instrument.
