Gen-7 corrections: significance-gated, estimator-matched labeler
(panel 2, rank 3) -- pre-registration 2026-09-04, declared before the
full mining run.

Premise (measured in the pilot): the gen-6 labeler (ungated argmax at
256 worlds) writes a correction whenever its search argmax differs from
the policy top.  30 c20 self-play games, 4,275 plies, 2,111 flagged,
1,139 corrected (54.0%, matching gen-6's 54.7%).  Of those 1,139:
  - 271 (24%) carried an exactly-zero lead: the labeler's symmetrized
    policy top differed from the raw argmax and the "correction" merely
    restated it (the miner compared against the raw top; fixed 41a2aa4:
    with a symmetrized labeler the reference is its own candidate 0).
  - the 868 search-driven ones: median lead 1.31 pts, median lead/SE
    0.77; only 40% clear 1 SE, 15% clear 2 SE, 13% reach 4 points.
So the median gen-6-style correction did NOT clear one paired SE at 256
worlds -- the corpus was mostly label noise around the policy top, which
is consistent with c19/c20 sliding after 1-2 iterations.
Mechanism: the labeler plays the deployed selection rule (sel_k gate,
sym_k/sym_bel 120, calibrated sampler, belief specialist) and a
correction is written (dup 4) only when the gated choice differs from
candidate 0 AND leads it by >= 4 points; states where the gated choice
is candidate 0 are confirmations (written once); gated leads under the
floor are logged as hedges and not written.  mine --selk K --pfloor 4
--sym --log FILE.
Pilot arms (30 games each, fixed reference): k=1.0 and k=1.5.  Go rule

per 400 games); the premise test above already passed.

Trainer hygiene (tools/train.c, integrated): --holdout FRAC splits by a
deal key (FNV-1a over the stored deck order and round; every ply, both
perspectives and all dup copies of a deal land on one side -- a
per-sample split would have put 18% of corr20a's states on both sides);
per-iteration held-out policy CE and top-1 for CORRECTION samples and
for the rest, with the train-side numbers beside them; --freeze-rows
LO:HI for feature ablations (rows restored after each Adam step, since
weight decay would otherwise move zero-gradient rows).
Calibration on corr20a (gen-6 corrections + anchor, 10% held out): the
held-out correction CE is at its minimum BEFORE training (1.899) and
only rises (1.906 after 4 iterations at lr 1e-4, 1.917 at 12; 1.93 at
lr 1e-3) while the train-side CE falls (1.849 -> 1.788 / 1.653) and
anchor agreement erodes (98% -> 91% / 79%).  The gen-6 corrections had
no generalizable likelihood gain: the fine-tune memorized them, and the
per-iteration arena max was choosing among memorization noise.  This is
the same finding as the pilot's 0.77-SE median from the other side.
Checkpoint rule for gen-7 (declared now): train with --holdout 0.1;
proceed only if the held-out correction CE FALLS below its it0 value on
at least one iteration; take the checkpoint at the held-out CE minimum
(ties -> earlier) and confirm it with ONE 1000-game raw arena.
Independent confirmation (mine --confirm IN OUT, declared after the
distillation pre-test exposed the winner's curse, before the full
corpus finished): every correction is re-labeled with an independent
world stream; kept only if the fresh run makes the same gated choice
AND the stored move's fresh lead over candidate 0 is >= 2 points.  On
the pilot corpora: k=1.0 126 corrections -> 65 confirmed (51.6%; 60
flips, 1 floor); k=1.5 122 -> 59 (48.4%).  Fresh lead of the stored
move: median 3.79 vs the original 5.71 -- the selection inflation
measured directly; confirmed set median 5.78 (100% >= 2, 78% >= 4).
Survival by deck: 0-8 69%, 9-16 81%, 17+ 32% -- early-round labels are
the unstable ones.  The full corpus is confirmed the same way
(data/corr21.smp -> data/corr21c.smp) before training.
