Gated search distillation on the tournament agent's own play
(panel 2, rank 4) -- pre-test 2026-09-04, protocol from the plan.

Mechanism built (mine --all --spec STRING; diff kept as
data/probes/distill_mine_all_2026-09-04.diff, not integrated): the
tournament agent plays both seats from ply 14 (raw policy sampled
before), the gen-7 labeler (256 worlds, sel_k 1, 4-point floor,
symmetrized, calibrated sampler) labels every searched ply; one-hot
(dup 2) only where the gated choice differs from candidate 0 and clears
the floor, self-target (the net's symmetrized top-12) elsewhere;
draw-only qualifiers rewritten as self-targets.
Pre-test (20 matches, seed 20260906, one thread, 2,026 searched plies):
  qualifier rate per searched ply 3.4%      (band 5-20%: below)
  share of qualifiers >= 2 SE 65%           (>= 50%: pass)
  draw-only share of qualifiers 30%          (< 5%: miss)
  plies per match 143.3                      (unchanged: pass)
  relabel stability, 50 qualifiers on a fresh world stream: 25 flipped
  (50% +/- 7%), 20 of them back to candidate 0; only 18 (36%) re-qualify
  under the floor                            (KILL if > 35%: KILLED)
  classes: deckburn and wclutch nearly vanish on the deployed agent's
  own distribution (0.25 and 0.05 per game vs 0.93 and 0.33 in raw
  self-play) -- the search already avoids those states at play time.
Verdict: NO-GO.  The finding that matters beyond this arm: at 256
worlds the 4-point floor admits leads of 4-6 points whose own SE is
~2, and selecting the best of five candidates inflates the winner's
lead, so half the qualifiers are selection on noise.  The same regime
produced the gen-7 corpus (median lead/SE 2.8 in its pilot vs 2.3
here).  Remedy declared for gen-7 before training: an independent
confirmation pass (mine --confirm) re-labels every correction with a
fresh world stream and keeps it only if the fresh run makes the same
gated choice with a lead >= 2 points.
