Prior-aware override threshold calibration -- 2026-08-24
========================================================

Question (user-posed): how much better must a move score in search,
relative to the policy's top choice, before the search should overrule
the policy -- as a function of the PRIOR GAP and the PLY.

Method: 640 self-play games (320 temperature-1 sampled trajectories,
320 policy-argmax trajectories), 15% of decision points sampled at
every ply, ~13k states / 93,191 candidate rows.  Each candidate scored
by (a) the exact play-time estimator: 96 paired worlds, deterministic
playouts, and (b) an oracle: 1024 worlds, sampled playouts.  Threshold
m*(gap, ply) = cheap edge at which E[oracle edge] crosses zero, from
the per-bucket regression E[dm1024 | dm96].  Primary fit restricted to
deployment-eligible candidates (rank<=4, prior>=0.02) per adversarial
review; log-linear form m* = lambda(ply) * log(p_top/p_c) verified
against the bucket table (residuals <= ~0.2 pts everywhere).

Known limits: the oracle is a lower-noise estimate of the SAME
policy-conditioned estimand the play-time playouts price, so shared
systematic playout bias cancels in the regression but is not removed;
the match gate remains the arbiter of actual strength.
