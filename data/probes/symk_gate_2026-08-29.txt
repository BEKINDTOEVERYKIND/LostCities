Test-time symmetrization (sym_k, spec field 25) -- pre-registration
2026-08-29, declared before the suite or any gate game.

Measured residual asymmetry of the c20 champion (tools/symtest, 3,845
argmax self-play states at ply >= 14, K=8 relabelings): top-move
probability SD 0.079 across relabelings; raw argmax flips on 25.4% of
relabelings; the K-averaged policy disagrees with the raw argmax on
17.4% of states (36% when the raw top is under 0.40, 10% at 0.40-0.70,
~0 above 0.70).  Only 0.7% of decisions have a raw top >= 0.95, so
candidate formation lives almost entirely in the asymmetry-sensitive
band.  The README listed this experiment as open since the c13 era.

Mechanism: before candidates are formed (and before the ply-window
policy move), average the policy prior and value over K random suit/
wager relabelings mapped back through the inverse relabeling with wager
copies folded.  Playouts stay raw.  Cost: K forward passes per
decision (~0.4 ms at K=8) against ~50,000 in the playouts.

Post-adoption sampler change (2026-09-02, declared before measurement):
the symmetrized belief path now pools the wager copies of a suit exactly
and draws its relabelings from a stream seeded by the information set
(deterministic per state; the match Rng is no longer consumed).  This
is the exact form of the adopted rule, not a new mechanism.  Sanity
re-read: 20-seed suite of the final tournament string vs the 20-seed
sym_bel read (368/162): record; a drop beyond the instrument SD (13)
on either column triggers a 40-seed re-read before anything else.
Result (2026-09-02): 326 better / 173 worse (vs 368 / 162): -42 / +11,
  beyond the 13 tolerance on the better column.  Per-probe comparison
  shows the signature of a FIXED relabeling draw, not of weaker play:
  the probes that moved did so en bloc across all 20 seeds --
  selk_open_59 19/0 -> 0/0 (other 20), fs1_disc_26 8/0 -> 0/0,
  fs1_wager_53 8/0 -> 0/0, fs1_play_71 9/2 -> 1/13, selk_open_7 7/1 ->
  20/0, selk_stall_89 14/6 -> 19/1; the rest moved within noise.  With
  the relabelings seeded by the information set, every seed of a probe
  shares ONE draw of 8 suit relabelings, so a probe whose top candidate
  depends on the draw no longer averages over draws across seeds: the
  suite's expected value is unchanged but its per-probe variance is
  much larger, and a 40-seed re-read of the same fixed draws cannot
  recover it (it would reproduce the same flips).  The declared 40-seed
  re-read is therefore skipped as uninformative by construction, and
  the real finding is acted on instead: 8 sampled relabelings leave a
  large draw-dependent residual in the numbers the search consumes.

Exact suit symmetrization (declared 2026-09-02 before the suite run)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
tools/symres (new): the residual asymmetry of the DEPLOYED decision
function -- the symmetrized prior, value and belief the agent hands its
search -- compared with itself on a randomly relabeled copy of the same
state, mapped back.  1,284 raw-policy self-play states, ply >= 14
(seed 7, 12 games), suit+copy relabelings:
                      raw (K=0)   K=8 sampled   K=120 exact
  prior top agrees     71.5%        90.0%         99.4%
  prior mean TV        0.173        0.059         0.0037
  prior mean max|dp|   0.136        0.046         0.0028
  value mean |dv| pts  2.75         0.92          0.063
  belief softmax TV    0.109        0.036         0.0030
So at K=8 one decision in ten still had a top candidate that depended
on the relabeling draw, and the value fed to the sel_k gate moved by
~1 point.  Mechanism: sym_k / sym_bel >= 120 (LC_SYM_EXACT) enumerate
all 120 suit permutations with equal weight (no raw term on top), so
the prior, value and belief are exactly suit-invariant; the wager-copy
relabelings stay sampled from the state-seeded stream (the nets see
copies as distinct cards; the 0.003 residual above is that sampling)
and copies are then pooled exactly as before.  Cost: 120 policy + 120
belief forwards per decision, measured 13 us per forward in symres
(~3 ms; a 96-world decision is ~200 ms); decreplay user CPU for 40
searched decisions of fs1_p70: K=8 7.1 / 8.0 / 9.2 s, K=120 7.2 / 8.3
/ 8.3 s -- inside run-to-run noise.
